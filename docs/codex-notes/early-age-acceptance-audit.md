# Nomad/Farm acceptance starting points

Read-only audit during recovered batch4 validation, at integration tip 639a8803 (contains main 09659615). These are acceptance gaps and design/implementation differences to verify with owners, not new gameplay implementations or claims of a completed survival run.

- `ages.py`: Nomad exits through Homestead Hearth; Farm exits by lighting Crucible Kiln. Read `quests2.py`, actual quests.ron, recipes.ron, cooking.ron, multiblocks.ron and server/hearth.rs.
- Actual Hearth uses a hearthstone in eight stone-tag blocks with two chimney blocks at a corner. Recipes are Construction 1 with cobbles/clay; this is newer implemented tuning than wiki Mining 5 dressed-stone wording. `base:light` checks the standing structure, assigns home, and tallies Built. Bedding is a quest prerequisite (Craft sleeping_bag), not a physical ingredient in the structure. Verify actual bedding use, home safety, destruction, death and saved reopening.
- Existing `a_lit_homestead_hearth_is_home_and_the_dead_wake_there` uses admin `/fill`, `/setblock`, `/tp` and `/kill`. It proves authority/structure/respawn, not fresh-survival attainability or a combined saved reopen path. The real game workshop also debug-builds gate structures. Both must be supplemented for new acceptance.
- Current Farm route is Potter -> Charcoal -> Red Metal -> Crucible Kiln. `food_enough` from wiki Q2 is absent from actual quests. Fresh acceptance must nevertheless sustain crops/animals/food, with gameplay/UI agreeing how it is guided.
- Hand smelting copper at Engineering 5 is an actual early route (ore + coal/charcoal). It avoids requiring the later Kiln before the Red Metal prerequisite. Charcoal rewards Engineering 325 and Mining 300; Red Metal rewards Engineering 750, so test that natural route earns the levels for crucible Engineering 10 without debug skill setting.
- Actual Kiln is twelve kiln-brick blocks around the crucible (eight ring + four upper corners); the crucible recipe costs seven more kiln bricks and a clay pot. The light action checks geometry and tallies Built, and machine use also tallies a completed multiblock once for its builder regardless of whether that use starts fuel or processing (`machines.rs:345–358`). Verify that the Farm transition really requires the promised lighting/operation. It does not implement the wiki's fuel/draught gate parts. Cooking validates standing structure. Gameplay/design must align the supported route and advertised text before acceptance; no duplicate machinery will be introduced here.
- Nomad First Night currently counts Cook(any), not explicitly craft campfire + cook food. Farm Potter counts Craft(clay_pot,6). Acceptance should use the intended actual early actions and inspect rewards/unlock order, rather than assuming wiki definitions are active.
