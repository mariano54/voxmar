## Current supported material chain

[Recipes](/Users/mariano54/Projects/voxmar-wt/loop-next/assets/content/base/recipes.ron:20): Hearth core8cobbled + two chimneys12cobbled/4clay + ring8stone = **28 stone/cobbled units and4clay**, excluding table/bedding. Pit kiln consumes a campfire (2logs/2cobblestone) +4cobbled. Six pots require3batches/15successful fired clay; one of those pots can be consumed by the crucible. The crucible needs7kiln-brick blocks and the surrounding pattern12 more; at4fired clay per brick block, Potter + Kiln require **91successful fired-clay outputs** (15+76), excluding cracking losses. Crafting thresholds: pots2/bricks5; crucible Engineering10. Copper's grid smelting consumes one coal or charcoal per ore; three ingots make the pickaxe. Current Kiln cooking consumes its ore/blend, but no fuel. These are source-derived minimums, not measured pacing. Clay is an underwater patch in the current base biome surface rules (biomes.ron55/97); coal/copper samples lie on open natural ground above veins and drop their ore (blocks.ron346/347). Trace safe clay collection and enough actual samples/vein access rather than assuming a wetland-bank or surface-copper shortcut.

Supported early food alternatives are level-one rabbit/chicken/egg/shrimp/minnow cooking and rod fishing. Wheat is Farming1 (12min base growth), rye3 and carrot5; a second crop type is a real prerequisite to trace for F01. Three bag recipes require Crafting5/5/1 respectively; shears are early stone tools, and mouflon taming is Hunter5. Salvage/trader Hearth parts, hearth pebbles, mud bricks, straw pallet, kiln clay/bone-ash, dung/fish-oil fuel and windy-ridge/tonic draught remain design promises without verified current routes. Do not queue them all; first complete one disclosed main path, then any alternate actually advertised.
